Four things. Done properly.

Websites Made to measure

Fast, phone-first sites that show the menu, the vibe, the hours and a WhatsApp button, because that's what people actually look for. Each one is designed from your place: your logo colours, your photos, the way you talk on Instagram.

Menus Engineering + design

Every menu has stars: dishes people love that also make you money. We cost each dish, check what really sells, and redesign the menu so your stars get the best spot and your puzzles get a push. The same costing discipline used in luxury hotel kitchens, sized for an independent café.

      Two conversations I still carry.

      Jaipur Literature Festival, January 2026

      I went to volunteer. I came back with a lesson in hosting.

      For five days, JLF turns Jaipur into one enormous living room. I signed up as a volunteer, mostly to be close to it. On one of those days I got to talk with Sanjoy K. Roy, the producer who has spent years building the festival.

      I expected a polite hello. What I got was a real conversation: curious, sharp and generous. In the middle of a festival with thousands of guests, he spoke as if there was nowhere else he needed to be.

      What I took homeThe bigger the room, the more each guest should feel it was set just for them.

      On the floor, Tata Starbucks

      Apron on, mid-shift, talking coffee with the CEO.

      A busy day, the bar running, my green apron already wearing the shift. That's when I met Sushant Dash, CEO of Tata Starbucks, right there on the floor.

      We talked about coffee, about people, about what makes a café somewhere you want to come back to. It was warm and genuinely thoughtful, and it reminded me that the people at the top of great brands still care about the cup in your hand.

      What I took homeGreat brands are built one cup, and one guest, at a time.

      Two very different rooms, one shared belief: start with the guest.

      things people ask

      Good questions.

      How long does a website take?

      A one-page site usually takes one to two weeks once we have your photos and menu. Bigger sites take longer, and we'll tell you the date in the quote.

      Can you take online orders on the site?

      We don't build shops or checkouts. We link straight to Zomato, Swiggy, Deliveroo, GrabFood or whatever you already use, so the site stays fast and simple to run.

      I don't have good photos. Is that a problem?

      Usually not. Your Instagram is often enough to start. If the photos really can't carry it, we'll tell you plainly and plan around it.

      What do you need from me to get started?

      Your logo, your menu, a handful of photos (your Instagram is often enough) and your opening hours. We take it from there and come back with a first draft.

      How do payments work?

      Half to start and half when the work is approved and ready to go live. Every project gets a fixed quote first, so the number never moves.

      What happens after we launch?

      We're still a WhatsApp away. Small fixes right after launch are on us, and later updates like a new menu or seasonal prices are quick and quoted upfront.
