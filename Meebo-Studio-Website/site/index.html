things people ask

Good questions.

How long does a website take?

A one-page site usually takes one to two weeks once we have your photos and menu. Bigger sites take longer, and we'll tell you the date in the quote.

Can you take online orders on the site?

We don't build shops or checkouts. We link straight to Zomato, Swiggy, Deliveroo, GrabFood or whatever you already use, so the site stays fast and simple to run.

I don't have good photos. Is that a problem?

Usually not. Your Instagram is often enough to start. If the photos really can't carry it, we'll tell you plainly and plan around it.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your logo, your menu, a handful of photos (your Instagram is often enough) and your opening hours. We take it from there and come back with a first draft.

How do payments work?

Half to start and half when the work is approved and ready to go live. Every project gets a fixed quote first, so the number never moves.

What happens after we launch?

We're still a WhatsApp away. Small fixes right after launch are on us, and later updates like a new menu or seasonal prices are quick and quoted upfront.
